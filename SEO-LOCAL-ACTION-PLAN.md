# Local SEO Action Plan — Off-Site Work

Code cannot produce local rankings on its own. For a service-area contractor, the
Google Business Profile, real reviews, and consistent citations carry more weight for
map-pack visibility than anything on the website. This is the work only you can do.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile (highest priority)

- Use the exact business name: **Shipp-Mates Epoxy of the Ozarks**. Do not append keywords
  such as "Epoxy Flooring Lake of the Ozarks" — name keyword stuffing is a violation and
  can get the profile suspended.
- Primary category: **Flooring Contractor**.
- Secondary categories to consider: Concrete Contractor, Flooring Store (only if accurate),
  Commercial Flooring Contractor.
- Set it as a **service-area business** if customers do not visit you. Add the service areas
  matching the site: Osage Beach, Camdenton, Lake Ozark, Sunrise Beach, Laurie, Eldon,
  Versailles, Linn Creek and the surrounding Lake communities.
- Add every service individually (epoxy garage floors, polyaspartic coatings, acid staining,
  metallic epoxy, quartz broadcast, grind and seal, patio coatings, commercial floors,
  concrete repair) with a short honest description each.
- Add hours: Mon–Fri 9:00am–5:00pm.
- Upload **real before-and-after photos weekly**. Geotagging is not required; consistency is.
- Post a Google Business Profile update at least twice a month — a finished job, a colour, a
  seasonal note. Short is fine.
- Add the calculator URL (`/instant-quote.html`) as the appointment/quote link.
- Answer every question in the Q&A section yourself, accurately.

## 2. Reviews

- Ask **every** completed customer for an honest Google review. The single best time is the day
  the floor is finished, in person, while they are looking at it.
- Encourage customers to mention the service and their town naturally — "flake garage floor in
  Camdenton" — but never script it for them.
- **Never** offer discounts, cash, or gifts in exchange for reviews. This violates Google policy
  and FTC rules.
- Respond to every review, positive or negative, within a few days.
- Link the site's review CTA to your real Google review form.
- Once you have genuine reviews, tell your developer — `AggregateRating` schema becomes eligible
  at that point and not before.

## 3. Citations (NAP consistency)

Your Name, Address, Phone must be **byte-identical** everywhere. Decide now whether you are
"417-255-6890" or "417.255.6890" and use that one form forever.

Create or correct listings on:

- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places
- Apple Business Connect
- Facebook (already linked from the site)
- Yelp
- Better Business Bureau (if you join)
- Angi
- Houzz
- Local chamber of commerce (Lake Area, Camdenton, Osage Beach)
- Lake of the Ozarks local directories
- Local contractor directories
- Your coating manufacturer's "find an installer" directory — often the highest-value one and
  frequently missed

## 4. Local links and partnerships

Earn links from real relationships, not purchases:

- Lake-area chambers of commerce
- Home builders and remodellers you work with
- Real estate agents and property managers (rental garages are a real market for you)
- Marinas and boat storage operators
- Complementary trades — garage door, epoxy countertop, cabinet installers
- Your material suppliers
- Community organisations and sponsorships
- Commercial customers willing to feature the completed project

**Do not** buy bulk backlinks, use private blog networks, automated link building, or paid
directory blasts. These carry real penalty risk and are difficult to undo.

## 5. Content and project cadence

Publish **real project case studies**, not generic articles. One genuine project write-up with
your own photographs outperforms ten generic posts. Use `PROJECTS.template.json` to capture:

- City and county
- The actual problem with the slab
- Slab condition before
- Preparation performed
- Coating system and finish installed
- Install duration
- Real before and after photographs
- Maintenance guidance given
- Honest result, including anything that was harder than expected

Target roughly one real project post per month. That cadence, plus weekly GBP photos and steady
review collection, is what actually moves local rankings.

---

## Reality check

No code change guarantees a number-one ranking. The technical work in this repository removes
the obstacles and makes the site eligible to compete. Items 1, 2 and 3 above are what decide
whether you actually appear in the local map pack.
